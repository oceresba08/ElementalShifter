# ElementalShifter
Elemental Shifter is a 3D action game where players are a rolling sphere that absorbs environmental elements. They can transform into those elements, harnessing unique abilities such as fire, water, earth, and more. Certain elements can be combined to unlock even more powerful fusions. Throughout the game, players battle evolving Void enemies that absorb elements to strengthen their power and destroy the world. Dynamic weather conditions affect elemental abilities and availability, forcing the player to think more strategically. Across three mythologically inspired levels, players have to defeat enemies, manage limited elemental resources, and survive environmental hazards controlled by Greek gods.
